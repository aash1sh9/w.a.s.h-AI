# Product Scope

## Target Users
- Student communities
- College clubs
- Hackathon/project teams
- Startup teams
- Community managers
- Any group that receives high-volume chat messages

## Main User Problem
Important information is mixed with casual conversation, forcing users to manually scan large message histories.

## Value Proposition
W.A.S.H AI converts conversation overload into structured information users can understand and act on.

## Core Outputs
- Chat summary
- Important announcements
- Relevant mentions
- Action items

## Product Positioning
W.A.S.H AI is not just a chatbot. It acts as an **intelligence layer over group conversations**.
